Who We Are

India's Trusted Inspection Partner

SAVIK Global provides inspectors, expeditors, and manpower services to leading inspection companies around the globe. We represent renowned Third Party Inspection (TPI) agencies in India and carry out inspections all over the country on their behalf.

Our inspector teams are strategically located across all four zones of India — ensuring prompt deployment and comprehensive coverage wherever your project demands.

Through trusted enterprise partners, we extend our capabilities for overseas inspection requirements, making SAVIK Global a truly global partner.
